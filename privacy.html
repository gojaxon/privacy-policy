Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

Hi, thanks for reading this. I run Jman55555 and I wrote this policy myself to explain what info I hold when you play my game, and how you can get rid of it if you want. It covers everyone, no matter where you live.

What I collect

To make the game work and to keep your account going, I store a few things:

  • Your Meta User ID
  • Your username
  • Your game and account data
  • Basic usage info tied to your account

That's about it. I don't collect your movement data, your camera feed, or anything from your room.

What I do with it

I only use this stuff to run the game. Concretely, that means: logging you in, saving your progress and game settings, keeping your account working, and stopping cheaters or abusers from wrecking things. I don't sell your info and I don't use it for ads.

Who else sees it

Nobody, really. I don't sell your data. I only hand it over if a court or the law says I have to, or if a service I use to run the game needs it to do its job.

How it's stored

Your account and game data sits on the servers that run the game. I do my best to keep it safe, but no online service is 100% bulletproof, and I can't promise that.

Deleting your data

If you want your data gone, just email me at jman@outlook.com and I'll sort it out. Anyone can ask, from any country, at any time, and it won't cost you anything. You don't even need a reason. I'll delete or anonymize what I have on you as soon as I've checked it's really you asking.

You can also ask to see what I have or fix it if something's wrong. Same email address.

There are a couple of rare cases where I might not delete something right away — like if I'm legally required to keep it, or I need it to stop fraud or deal with a dispute. If that happens, I'll tell you exactly why.

How long I keep it

I keep your data as long as you have an account and the game needs it. When an account is deleted, I delete or anonymize what I can, apart from anything I'm legally obliged to hang onto or need for security or fraud reasons.

Kids

The game isn't meant to collect info from kids where that breaks the law. If you think that's happened, get in touch and I'll look into it.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of the page.
